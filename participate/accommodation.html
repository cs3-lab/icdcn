Getting Around

App Cabs

UBER, OLA and other app-based cab services operate in Mangalore. Reliable for airport transfers and city-to-NITK commutes at any time of day.

Auto-rickshaws

Readily available near Surathkal Railway Station and around NITK. Convenient for short distances within Surathkal.

City Buses

KSRTC and private buses connect Mangalore city to Surathkal regularly. Route 35A stops near the NITK gate.
