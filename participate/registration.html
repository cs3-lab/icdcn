ICDCN 2027 · NITK Surathkal, India · January 4-7, 2027

Main Conference Papers October 20, 2026 (AoE)
Workshop / Demo / Poster November 14, 2026 (AoE)
General Registration December 5, 2026 (AoE)

Registration Instructions

1

Main Conference Papers

  • At least one author of each accepted Main Conference paper must register at the Regular Registration rate, even if the presenting author is a student.
  • Student Registration is applicable only to student attendees and authors of accepted Doctoral Symposium papers, provided they do not have any accepted papers in the Main Conference or any other track requiring Regular Registration.
2

Multiple Main Conference Papers

  • For authors with multiple accepted Main Conference papers, the first paper must be registered at the Regular Registration rate.
  • The second and more papers must be registered at the One-Day Registration rate for each.
3

Workshop, Demo, and Poster Papers

  • At least one author must register at the One-Day Registration rate if attending only on the day of their presentation (excluding banquet).
  • Authors wishing to attend the entire conference (January 4–7, 2027) must register at the Regular Registration rate.
4

Student Authors of Workshop / Demo / Poster Papers

  • Applicable only to student attendess and all authors of the paper should be students.
  • A student who is the sole registrant for a Workshop, Demo, or Poster paper and wishes to attend the full conference must:
Register the paper at the One-Day Registration rate
Additionally purchase a Student Registration to attend all four conference days
5

Doctoral Symposium Papers

  • Exclusively for student authors. Doctoral Symposium papers may be registered at the Student Registration rate.

Benefits Under Each Category

Full Conference Registration

Indian & International Regular (ACM & Non-Member) · Indian & International Student (ACM & Non-Member)

  • Access to all sessions (Jan 4–7) including keynotes, tutorials, workshops, and demos
  • Coffee breaks and lunches on all four days
  • Conference Banquet ticket (1 included)
  • Conference Kit
  • Access to online technical proceedings

One Day Registration

Indian & International Regular One Day · Indian & International Student One Day

  • Access to all sessions for one day including keynotes, tutorials, workshops, and demos
  • Coffee breaks and lunch on the registered day
  • Conference Kit
  • Conference Banquet not included
