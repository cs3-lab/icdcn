Workshops at ICDCN 2027

Workshop Submissions — must comply with the Generative AI Guidelines, adhere to the Double-Blind Review Policy, and not exceed 6 pages.

At least one author of each accepted Workshop, Demo, or Poster paper must register at the appropriate rate:

  • Workshop-Day Registration — required if attending only on the day of your presentation.
  • Regular Registration — required if attending the full conference (January 4–7, 2027).

Important Dates (All the deadlines are in AoE)

Submission portal opens August 10, 2026
Paper submission deadline September 1, 2026
Acceptance notification October 15, 2026
Camera-Ready Submission October 29, 2026
Workshop during ICDCN 2027 January 4-7, 2027
